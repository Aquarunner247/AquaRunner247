# Data Processing Agreement

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement (“**DPA**”) forms part of, and is incorporated into, the Terms of Service or other written agreement governing the use of the AquaRunner247 services (the “**Agreement**”) between **AquaRunner247 LLC**, with its address at **732 S 6th St, Ste N, Las Vegas, NV 89101** (“**AquaRunner247**”), and the customer identified in the Agreement (“**Customer**”). This DPA applies when AquaRunner247 Processes Personal Data on Customer’s behalf.

AquaRunner247 and Customer may each be called a “**Party**” and together the “**Parties**.” Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **“Applicable Data Protection Law”** means all data protection and privacy laws applicable to the Processing under this DPA, including, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“**CCPA**”), other applicable U.S. state privacy laws, the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

1.2 **“Controller”** means the person or entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data, or the equivalent term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.3 **“Data Subject”** means an identified or identifiable individual to whom Personal Data relates, or the equivalent term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.4 **“EU GDPR”** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

1.5 **“Personal Data”** means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, or the equivalent term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.6 **“Personal Data Breach”** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data Processed under this DPA.

1.7 **“Process”** or **“Processing”** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including its collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, retrieval, alteration, restriction, deletion, or destruction.

1.8 **“Processor”** means a person or entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller, or the equivalent term under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.9 **“Restricted Transfer”** means a transfer of Personal Data subject to restrictions on international transfers under Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.10 **“Security Incident”** means a confirmed breach of AquaRunner247’s security that results in accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Customer Personal Data. A failed or unsuccessful attempt that does not compromise Customer Personal Data is not, by itself, a Security Incident.

1.11 **“Standard Contractual Clauses” or “SCCs”** means the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, as amended or replaced, and, where applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

1.12 **“Subprocessor”** means a third party engaged by AquaRunner247 to Process Customer Personal Data on AquaRunner247’s behalf in connection with the Services.

1.13 **“Supervisory Authority”** means an independent public authority responsible for enforcing Applicable Data Protection Law.

1.14 **“UK GDPR”** means the UK General Data Protection Regulation, as it forms part of the law of the United Kingdom.

## 2. Scope, Roles, and Term

2.1 **Scope.** This DPA applies to AquaRunner247’s Processing of Personal Data on behalf of Customer through the Services. The nature, purposes, categories of Personal Data, categories of Data Subjects, and frequency of Processing are described in **Annex 1**.

2.2 **Roles; dual framing.** For Personal Data Customer or its authorized users submit to the Services about Customer’s technicians, staff, End Customers, properties, pools, and operations, Customer acts as Controller or business and AquaRunner247 acts as Processor or service provider. This DPA is intended to satisfy both the GDPR Article 28 processor-term requirements and the CCPA service-provider contract-term requirements: AquaRunner247 processes Customer Personal Data only on Customer’s documented instructions, does not sell or share it as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and does not retain, use, or disclose it for any purpose other than providing the Services, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law. The Parties acknowledge that their roles may differ for particular Processing where Applicable Data Protection Law so requires. Nothing in this DPA makes AquaRunner247 the Controller of Personal Data that AquaRunner247 Processes solely on Customer’s documented instructions.

2.3 **Term.** This DPA takes effect on the date Customer accepts the Agreement or otherwise agrees to use the Services, and continues for as long as AquaRunner247 Processes Personal Data on Customer’s behalf.

2.4 **Customer’s customers.** Customer is responsible for determining its role and obligations where it Processes Personal Data on behalf of its own customers or other third parties. Customer must ensure that its agreements and instructions authorize the Processing and permit Customer to provide the instructions required under this DPA.

## 8. International Transfers

8.1 **Processing locations; map providers.** AquaRunner247 stores Customer Personal Data in the United States, and all of its hosting, database, storage, email, telephony, payment, monitoring, and AI Subprocessors Process it in the United States.

Three map-related Subprocessors identified in Annex 3 operate from Europe: geocoding, driving-route calculation, and street map imagery. When a Customer or its users geocode an address, optimize a route, or view a map, **property street addresses and the map coordinates of service stops are transmitted from the United States to those providers in Europe**. No other category of Customer Personal Data is sent to them. These are transfers out of the United States rather than Restricted Transfers out of the EEA or the UK, so no Article 46 transfer mechanism is engaged; they are disclosed here because they are material to Customer’s own records of where its data goes.

AquaRunner247 will not make a Restricted Transfer of Customer Personal Data unless the transfer is permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law and subject to an appropriate transfer mechanism. If Processing or Subprocessor arrangements change such that a Restricted Transfer would occur, the Parties will implement a lawful transfer mechanism before the transfer occurs.

8.2 **EU transfers.** Where the EU GDPR applies and a Restricted Transfer is made from the European Economic Area to a country not recognized as providing an adequate level of protection, the Parties incorporate the EU Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 by reference. The Parties select **Module Two: Controller to Processor**. The SCCs apply as follows:

a. the data exporter is Customer, acting as Controller, and the data importer is AquaRunner247, acting as Processor;

b. the optional docking clause in Clause 7 is included;

c. the competent supervisory authority, governing law, and courts will be completed as follows: **[Competent Supervisory Authority]**, **[EU Member State law]**, and **[EU Member State courts]**, respectively, in accordance with the SCCs and Applicable Data Protection Law;

d. Annex I of the SCCs is completed using the Parties’ details and the Processing details in Annex 1 to this DPA; and

e. Annex II of the SCCs is completed using the security measures in Annex 2 to this DPA.

The SCCs are incorporated without modification, except for completing the appendices and selections permitted by the SCCs. If this DPA conflicts with the SCCs, the SCCs prevail to the extent of the conflict.

8.3 **UK transfers.** Where the UK GDPR applies and a Restricted Transfer is made from the United Kingdom, the Parties incorporate the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (“UK Addendum”) by reference, completed using the information in this DPA and Annexes 1–3. The UK Addendum is to be used with the EU SCCs described in Section 8.2, with Module Two selected. The UK Addendum’s mandatory text will not be modified, and its tables and required selections will be completed before the transfer. If the UK Addendum conflicts with this DPA, the UK Addendum prevails to the extent of the conflict.

8.4 **Transfer assessments and supplementary measures.** The Parties will cooperate in good faith, as required by Applicable Data Protection Law, to assess Restricted Transfers and implement any supplementary measures reasonably necessary for the transfer. Customer will provide relevant information about the transfer and its regulatory context; AquaRunner247 will provide reasonably available information about its Processing and security measures.

## 9. Return and Deletion of Personal Data

9.1 **Export and deletion.** On termination or expiration of the Agreement, Customer may export Customer Personal Data using available Service functionality or request a copy where reasonably available. Customer Personal Data will remain available for export for **30 days** after termination or expiration. Subject to Sections 9.2 and 9.5, AquaRunner247 will delete or return Customer Personal Data, at Customer’s choice and to the extent technically feasible, within **30 days** after termination or expiration.

AquaRunner247 also takes an automatic archive of Customer Personal Data when it detects termination, as a safeguard against Customer losing records it has not yet exported. That archive is held in encrypted storage, is not used for any other purpose, and is deleted on the same schedule as the data it copies.

9.2 **Backups and legal retention.** Copies retained in routine backups will be deleted or overwritten in accordance with AquaRunner247’s ordinary backup cycle, which will not exceed **90 days** after the applicable deletion date, unless retention for a longer period is required by law. Any retained data will remain protected under this DPA and will not be Processed except as required by law or for secure backup, restoration, or compliance purposes.

9.3 **Certification.** On Customer’s written request, AquaRunner247 will provide written confirmation of deletion or return of Customer Personal Data, subject to any retention permitted under Section 9.2.

9.4 **Customer’s responsibility.** Customer is responsible for exporting data it needs before access to the Services ends and for ensuring that its own copies, exports, and records are deleted or retained lawfully.

9.5 **Compliance records retained after termination.** Health-department compliance logging for a body of water attaches to that body of water rather than to the service company recording it, and the Services publish those logs at a QR code posted at the pool. So notwithstanding Section 9.1, AquaRunner247 retains the following after termination, without a fixed end date:

a. water-reading logs, contamination incident records, and inspection reports;

b. the customer name, property, body-of-water, and service-visit records needed to identify and display those logs; and

c. customer portal accounts (an end customer’s name and email address), which are blocked from signing in but not deleted, so that an end customer can retain their own identity and records if they later contract with AquaRunner247 or another Organization directly.

On termination AquaRunner247 clears the operational and personal fields from the records retained under (b) — the technician assigned to each visit, technician notes, arrival GPS coordinates, and Customer’s own notes and billing reference for a customer. Everything outside (a) to (c) is deleted on the Section 9.1 schedule.

Customer acknowledges this retention and instructs AquaRunner247 to carry it out as part of the Services. A Party may ask the other to delete a specific retained record, and AquaRunner247 will comply unless retention is required by law or necessary to keep a regulatory log intact. Customer is responsible for telling the individuals whose Personal Data this concerns, and for confirming the retention is consistent with its own obligations.

# Annex 1 — Details of Processing

## 1. Subject Matter

AquaRunner247’s provision of an all-in-one pool service route management and compliance mobile application and related portals, reporting, and support services to Customer.

## 2. Duration

For the term of the Agreement and any period during which AquaRunner247 retains Customer Personal Data in accordance with Section 9 of this DPA or as required by law.

## 3. Nature and Purpose of Processing

AquaRunner247 Processes Customer Personal Data as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and support the Services, including to:

- create and administer user accounts and permissions;
- manage route schedules, service visits, properties, and bodies of water;
- record and display arrival, departure, and visit information, including the geolocation fix captured at the moment of logged arrival at a service visit, timestamps, and photos taken during service visits. While a technician has an unfinished route open in the mobile app, the app reads device location continuously — including in the background, with background location permission — in order to detect arrival at a stop and departure from a stop that was not finished; only the fix that records an arrival is transmitted to AquaRunner247 and stored, a departure is transmitted as a time with no position attached, and the intervening positions are evaluated on the device and discarded;
- record water chemistry readings, dosage information, inspection checklists, and compliance records;
- display records through the customer portal for End Customers, inspector portals, reports, and pump room QR codes as configured or directed by Customer;
- provide route optimization and mobile app functionality, including offline data queuing and synchronization;
- extract structured equipment and inspection information from uploaded inspection reports;
- handle, record, and transcribe inbound calls to a Customer’s configured phone number and process call transcripts into structured service requests using the subprocessors listed in Annex 3;
- provide customer support, troubleshoot, and secure the Services; and
- perform other Processing documented in Customer’s use and configuration of the Services.

AquaRunner247 does not determine Customer’s purposes for the Personal Data Customer enters into the Services.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data

Depending on Customer’s use and configuration of the Services, Personal Data may include:

- technician and staff account details, such as names, work contact details, usernames, roles, and account activity;
- property, business, and individual contact details;
- location and geolocation information, including geofenced arrival records, departure times, and visit locations;
- geotagged and timestamped photos of pools, equipment, properties, and service visits;
- chemical readings, dosage records, service notes, and visit histories;
- state or local compliance inspection records, checklists, and related service documentation;
- equipment make, model, and serial number information extracted from uploaded reports, where associated with an identifiable person or property;
- call recordings and transcripts for inbound calls to a Customer’s configured phone number, including caller phone numbers and call content;
- technician GPS location captured at the moment of logged arrival at a service visit (AquaRunner247 stores the arrival fix only; a departure is stored as a time without a position, as described in Section 3 of this Annex);
- photos taken during service visits, including geotagged and timestamped images;
- gate codes, entry details, and access instructions;
- records shared with or made available to customers, inspectors, property managers, or others through customer portals, reports, QR codes, or email; and
- technical and usage information associated with accounts and use of the Services.

The Service is not designed to collect Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, health information, or similarly sensitive categories, and Customer agrees not to input such data into the Service. Customer will not intentionally submit special categories of Personal Data or highly sensitive Personal Data unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 5. Categories of Data Subjects

- Customer’s employees, technicians, contractors, and other authorized users;
- Customer’s residential customers and their household or property contacts;
- employees, agents, residents, tenants, guests, or other contacts associated with commercial properties serviced by Customer;
- property managers, inspectors, and other individuals whose information Customer enters into or makes available through the Services; and
- other individuals whose Personal Data Customer or its users submit to the Services.

## 6. Frequency of Processing and Transfers

Processing and transfers occur on a continuous, recurring, or event-driven basis as Customer and its users access and use the Services, including when they enter, upload, view, disclose, synchronize, or export information.

## 7. Processing Locations

Customer Personal Data is stored in the United States and Processed there by AquaRunner247 and by all of its authorized Subprocessors except the three map-related providers identified in Annex 3, which operate from Europe and receive only property street addresses and the map coordinates of service stops. See Section 8.1.

# Annex 2 — Technical and Organizational Security Measures

AquaRunner247 will maintain technical and organizational measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data. The measures applicable to the Services include the following, as appropriate to the Processing and risks:

## 1. Encryption in Transit and at Rest

- Use encryption in transit for Personal Data transmitted between users’ devices, the Services, and applicable Subprocessors.
- Use encryption at rest for Personal Data stored in systems where available and appropriate.
- Restrict access to encryption keys and related secrets to authorized personnel and systems.

## 2. Access Controls and Least Privilege

- Limit access to Customer Personal Data to personnel and systems that require access for authorized business purposes.
- Use role-based permissions and authentication controls for administrative access.
- Review access rights periodically and revoke access when no longer required.
- Protect credentials and secrets through appropriate handling and storage practices.

## 3. Device and Session Management

- Use reasonable controls for administrative devices and user sessions, including session expiration or revocation where supported.
- Apply access controls to devices used by authorized personnel to administer or support the Services.
- Provide customers with account and user management features where available; Customer remains responsible for managing its users’ access.

## 4. Logging and Monitoring

- Collect server request logs, application error reports, and performance traces through the monitoring Subprocessors identified in Annex 3, and retain them under a rolling window of 90 days or less.
- Rely on the authentication, database, and platform logs maintained by the hosting and authentication Subprocessors identified in Annex 3 for records of administrative and infrastructure events.
- Restrict access to those logs to authorized personnel through the relevant provider’s access controls.
- Use monitoring and alerting designed to surface errors and anomalies and to support investigation of security events.

AquaRunner247 does not currently maintain an in-application audit trail of end-user actions within a Customer’s account, and does not represent that one exists. Where Customer requires such a trail, the Parties will address it in an Order.

## 5. Geofence and Photo Integrity

- Use application functionality to associate supported visit records and photos with relevant timestamps and location information.
- Apply reasonable controls to preserve the integrity of records as captured or synchronized through the Services.
- Use blur detection or similar functionality where available to flag potentially unclear photos; such functionality does not guarantee that a photo is accurate, complete, or acceptable for regulatory purposes.

## 6. Offline Queue Protections

- Use reasonable safeguards for Personal Data temporarily queued on a device while offline, including protections available through the mobile operating system and application.
- Synchronize queued records when connectivity returns, subject to normal technical limitations.
- Customer will instruct its users to protect devices, enable appropriate device security, and report lost or compromised devices promptly.

## 7. Backup and Disaster Recovery

- Maintain backups and recovery procedures designed to support restoration of the Services and Customer Personal Data.
- Restrict access to backups and apply appropriate security protections.
- Periodically assess backup and recovery processes, as appropriate to the Services.

## 8. Vulnerability and Patch Management

- Maintain processes designed to identify, assess, and address material vulnerabilities in systems used to provide the Services.
- Apply security updates and patches within a timeframe appropriate to the severity of the issue and operational risk.
- Use reasonable development and deployment practices to reduce security risks.

## 9. Personnel Training and Confidentiality

- Require personnel authorized to access Customer Personal Data to be subject to confidentiality obligations.
- Provide personnel with security and privacy training appropriate to their roles.
- Maintain procedures for reporting and responding to suspected security incidents.

## 10. Physical Security and Hosting
