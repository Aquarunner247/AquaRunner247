## 1. Introduction and Scope

AquaRunner247 (“**AquaRunner247**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) provides software and related services for pool service and maintenance businesses. Our services include route management, residential and commercial pool records, state-specific inspection checklists, chemical logs, QR-code access to pool records, geolocation-based arrival tracking, photo-based visit documentation, dosage calculations, document scanning, customer and inspector portals, and related support (collectively, the “**Services**”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process Personal Information through our website at **aquarunner247.com**, our mobile application, portals, communications, and other Services.

The Services are intended primarily for businesses and their personnel. Pool service companies and other business customers may enter information about their technicians, customers, properties, property owners or managers, and other individuals. If you are an individual whose information a customer has submitted to the Services, that customer may control how your information is collected and used. See Section 11.

This Policy does not replace any separate privacy notice or data processing agreement that applies to a particular Service or relationship. If a separate agreement conflicts with this Policy regarding information we process on a customer’s behalf, that agreement will govern to the extent of the conflict.

## 7. Location, Photos, and Document Scanning

### 7.1 Location and background location

Some features use location information to support routes, geofenced arrival logging, and visit documentation. Depending on the feature, your device settings, and permissions you grant, the app may collect location information while the app is in use and, if background location permission is enabled, while the app is running in the background or closed. Location collection may not function as intended if permissions are denied, device settings are changed, or location services are unavailable.

You can generally disable or change location permissions through your device’s operating system settings. A Customer administrator may also control certain Service settings. Disabling location may limit or prevent geofenced arrival and related features, but does not necessarily remove location information already recorded in Customer Data.

### 7.2 Photos and files

When an End User takes or uploads a photo, the Services may associate it with a service visit and record the date, time, or location, depending on the device, app settings, and permissions. The Services may analyze photo quality, including detecting whether an image appears blurry. Photos can capture people or other information unintentionally; users should avoid including people or unnecessary sensitive details.

Customers and End Users may upload inspection reports and other documents. The document scanner may extract equipment make, model, and serial-number information or other information from those files. Extracted information may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed by the user. Customers are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to upload documents and information about others.

## 8. QR Codes, Portals, and Compliance Records

A Customer may create a QR code for a pool, spa, splash pad, fountain, or other body of water. Scanning the code may provide access to the records or information configured for that code, which may include time-stamped chemical logs, inspection histories, service information, or other compliance records. Depending on the Customer’s settings and the design of the feature, information may be available to people who scan the code, including inspectors, property managers, or others with access to the code or link.

Customers determine what records are associated with each QR code and are responsible for reviewing those records before making them accessible. Do not include information in a QR-linked record that should not be visible to a person who can scan or access the code. Portal access may also expose information to Customer-selected users, such as customers, property managers, or inspectors, according to the Customer’s access settings.

At the end of a subscription, Customer Data remains available for export for **30 days**, after which it is deleted or de-identified, except as described in the next paragraph or to the extent retention is required or permitted by law or is necessary for security, backup, or dispute-resolution purposes.

**What we keep indefinitely, and why.** Health-department compliance logging for a commercial pool or spa is tied to the body of water, not to whoever happens to be servicing it. If we deleted the water-reading history, contamination incident records, and inspection reports when an Organization cancelled, the QR-code log page posted at that pool would stop resolving and the record a regulator expects to find would disappear. So we retain those records, and the minimum customer, property, body-of-water, and service-visit rows needed to display them, without a fixed end date. When an Organization’s subscription ends we also clear the operational and personal fields from those retained records — the technician who performed each visit, their notes, the arrival GPS coordinates, and the Organization’s own notes and billing reference for a customer. Everything else is deleted. The applicable agreement or DPA may provide additional details, and an Organization that believes a particular record should not be retained can contact us at **privacy@aquarunner247.com**.
